# Roadmap 🗺️

Future plans for Kirtanam Sadhana.

## Version 2.0 (Planned)

### ☁️ Data Sync & Accounts (Optional)
- **User Accounts**: Optional login to sync progress across devices.
- **Cloud Backup**: Encrypted backup of notes and streaks.

### 📱 Mobile Experience
- ~~**PWA Support**: Full Progressive Web App support (Install on Home Screen).~~ ✅ Done — installable, offline browsing, lock-screen controls.
- **Offline Mode**: Ability to download lectures for offline listening.

### 🤝 Social & Community
- **Share Quotes**: Generate shareable images from lecture quotes.
- **Reading Groups**: Simple challenges/leaderboards (opt-in).

### 🎧 Enhanced Player
- **Playlists**: Create custom playlists (e.g., "Favorite Kirtans", "Morning Walk").
- **Transcription**: AI-generated transcriptions for searchability.
- **Sleep Timer**: Auto-stop playback.
